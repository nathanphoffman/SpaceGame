# core tasks (round 4)

Owns `server/Core/**`, `server/Core.Tests/**`, `server/Sim/**`. One item per Claude session. Core is pure C#: no I/O, no clock, dice and time are arguments.
- [ ] Enemy AI part 2a: attack choice in `EnemyBrain`: target the weakest visible-to-it player-held planet near its frontier when its fleet is at least 1.5x as strong (strength = expected damage per round times HP), otherwise wait and build. Deterministic tests with a seed.
- [ ] Enemy AI part 2b: Earth attack schedule: first no earlier than game hour 36 (fighters only), then every ~18 hours, each fleet about 1.35x the last, +25% per extra player. All numbers in `EnemyConfig`. Tests for the schedule and the scaling.
- [ ] Scoring: 1 point per game hour per held objective plus 1 on capture for both sides, `ScoreChanged` and `PlanetCaptured` events, scores in `GameState`. Tests, including a JSON round-trip.
- [ ] Endgame: 500-point win, end of week (tie goes to the enemy), instant win (team Colony ship claims the enemy home) and instant loss (enemy wins a battle at Earth). Emit `GameEnded` with data keys `winner`, `reason`, `teamScore`, `enemyScore`, `contributions` (JSON array of `{player, points}`), because the server's outcome endpoint reads those. Stop accepting commands after the end. Tests, including the ended state round-trip.
- [ ] World check (milestone b): a test that the enemy home trip at speed 1.0 takes 1 to 2 days and the nearest star about 4 hours; tune the constants in `TravelRules` if not. Also test the 10 objectives sit in 30% to 70% of the distance and in at least 6 systems.
- [ ] Full-week determinism test: run a seeded game for a week with no player actions through `Advance`; check the enemy colonizes, attacks Earth on schedule, the game ends, and the same seed gives the same result.
- [ ] Balance (milestone j): use `server/Sim` to print win rates for typical fleets (4 Fighters vs Battleship, Dreadnought vs Fighters, with and without Defense and Weapons upgrade); tune costs and enemy numbers in `GameConfig` so Earth attacks are beatable with sensible play; note the numbers in DECISIONS.md.
