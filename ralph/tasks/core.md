# core tasks (round 2)

Owns `server/Core/**`, `server/Core.Tests/**`, `server/Sim/**`. One item per Claude session. Core is pure C#: no I/O, no clock, dice and time are arguments.

- [ ] Quality: `server/Core` has 22 files in one folder. Group them into subfolders (e.g. `Rules/`, `Economy/`, `State/`, `Engine/`; keep the namespace `SpaceGame.Core`) so no folder has more than ~6 items. Also split `Core.Tests/CatalogTests.cs` `ShipsMatchSpec` (11 args) into a data-driven test with a small record. Run `codequality . --full` and fix the worst remaining findings in Core files (short names like `now`, magic numbers, long functions). Do not change public interfaces.
- [ ] Quality: split the biggest test classes (`BuildQueueTests` 22 methods, `UpgradeTests` 15) into several files of about 6 tests each, and flatten the deepest call chain (`Advance -> Complete -> CompletePlayer -> Finish -> Apply -> Build -> Lookup -> RatePerHourMilli ...`) by having callers use helpers directly or merging tiny pass-through functions.
- [ ] World data (milestone b): port the star catalog (read `lib/data`) into C# data in Core (about 24 systems closest to Sol with real Sol planets), with the 4-element richness (Fe+Si+Al+C -> Iron, O+H -> Hydrogen, Ti, U+He -> Helium-3). Earth rich in Iron, Hydrogen, Titanium and nearly no Helium-3; gas giants have Helium-3. Plain data records, no engine wiring yet. Tests for the conversion and for Earth and the gas giants.
- [ ] `GalaxyGenerator.Generate(seed)`: the ~24 systems, enemy home = farthest system from Sol with its biggest planet as the home world, 10 objective planets in systems at 30% to 70% of the distance to enemy home, spread over at least 6 systems; deterministic from the seed. Tests for each rule and for determinism. Add `GalaxyMap` content (systems, planets, richness, objective flags) in `Views.cs` by adding fields only.
- [ ] Wire the galaxy into `NewGame`: real Earth, every player's Earth holding with the starting kit, enemy home owned by the enemy. Replace the placeholder planet and the fixed 1 AU in `Quote` with real distances: `TravelRules` in-system (AU) and between-system (light years squared), tuned so the nearest star is about 4 hours and the enemy home 1 to 2 days at speed 1.0. `PlayerView` gets own planets and holdings (add fields only). `Apply(CancelQueueItem)` with refund. Tests, including JSON round-trip.
- [ ] Fleets part 1 (milestone d): `Apply(SendFleet)` (ships taken from the planet, cargo for Haulers, validation messages), `Apply(RecallFleet)`, fleets in `GameState` with `LaunchAt`, `DepartAt`, `ArriveAt`; `Advance` moves them and resolves arrivals in time order; `PlayerView.Fleets` carries what the map needs. Tests for prep time, flight, recall using time already flown.
- [ ] Fleets part 2: battles at arrival through `BattleResolver` (defenders = owner side plus allied ships, Defense building bonus, damage persists, healing at a shipyard over an hour), `BattleReport` records and `GameEvent`s, `Colonize` (unowned or enemy planet with no enemy ships, destroys buildings), Hauler trade (cargo unloads into the stockpile). Tests with scripted dice.
- [ ] Visibility (fog of war): `ViewFor` shows own fleets and planets and enemy fleets only within one hop of any team planet or fleet (3 nearest neighbours; Scouts 2 hops), reveals target and arrival time when spotted, and emits `FleetSpotted` once per fleet. Tests for hop distances and the Scout range.
